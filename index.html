Our Story

A Legacy of Craftsmanship and Care

Founded in 2016 in the heart of Standard City, Glamour Studio has redefined what it means to visit a salon. We believe every visit should feel like a mini retreat — a sanctuary where skilled artistry meets genuine hospitality.

Our unisex philosophy means everyone deserves the same level of exceptional care. Whether you're a gentleman seeking a refined cut or a woman looking for a complete transformation, our team delivers beyond expectations.
